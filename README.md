# AI Meeting Notes

AI Meeting Notes summarizes internal meetings, extracts decisions, and creates action items.

## Team Rules

- Internal prompt changes can be merged after CI passes and one review.
- Meeting transcripts can be shared inside PRIDE, but external demos must anonymize attendee names and customer company names.
- Action item extraction should preserve owner, due date, and decision context.
- “다음 주 초” means Monday morning unless Kim says otherwise.
- Summary changes should be tested on at least 10 sample meetings before release.
- UI-only note formatting changes do not require Kim's approval.
